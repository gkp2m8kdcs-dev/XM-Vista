Privacy Promise

Your data lives on your device. Period.

XM Vista was built on a simple principle: a personal investment journal should not need to phone home. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics SDKs of any kind.

  • ✓No login or sign-up required
  • ✓No analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, no crash reporting services
  • ✓No brokerage integrations, no order routing, no trade execution
  • ✓All portfolio data is stored in a local JSON snapshot on your iPhone
  • ✓One-tap CSV export whenever you want to leave
