1. Summary at a Glance

XM Vista ("the app") is an offline-first personal investment journal. We designed it so that your financial data never needs to leave your iPhone. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what we do — and, more importantly, what we don't.

In short: there is no account system, no cloud sync, no telemetry, and no behavioral profiling. The single source of truth for your data is a local JSON snapshot on your device.

2. Tracking Statement (App Store)

This section is provided in compliance with Apple's App Store privacy requirements and is intended to be a definitive statement about tracking in XM Vista.

Tracking is defined as: the linking of user or device data with third-party data for advertising purposes, or sharing with a data broker for the purposes of cross-app or cross-site behavioral advertising, attribution, or fingerprinting.

XM Vista's Tracking Declaration:

  • We do not track you. The app contains no tracking code, no advertising SDKs, and no fingerprinting libraries.
  • We do not use the IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) or any equivalent identifier for tracking purposes.
  • We do not share user data with third parties for advertising, attribution, or analytics.
  • We do not link your activity in XM Vista with activity in other companies' apps or websites for any purpose.
  • We do not display any advertising within the app.
  • We do not request, access, or read your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) at any time.
  • We do not embed any third-party analytics, including but not limited to Firebase, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, Sentry, Crashlytics, or App Center.

Because XM Vista performs no tracking of any kind, there is no opt-in screen required and no setting to configure. The Tracking setting in iOS will show that XM Vista has "No Tracking Activity."

If we ever change this — for example, by adding optional analytics — we will update this section first, request consent, and surface an in-app notice before any tracking begins.

3. What Data XM Vista Collects

XM Vista collects, in total, the following categories of information:

Data YOU enter into the app (stored locally on your device):

  • Portfolio holdings: asset name, ticker, asset class, quantity, cost basis, currency
  • Transactions: date, type (buy / sell / dividend / split), price, quantity, fees, notes, tags
  • Receipts: merchant, date, amount, category, and any photos you attach
  • Goals: target amount, deadline, contributions, and progress
  • Calculator inputs and the educational articles you have read

Data we automatically collect: None.

We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, location data, crash logs, or usage telemetry. We do not know how often you open the app, which screens you visit, or how long you spend on a particular calculator.

Data we receive from Apple:

Apple may collect anonymized, aggregated App Store download statistics (for example, total downloads per country). This is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and not by XM Vista.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

All financial data you enter into XM Vista is written to a single local JSON snapshot inside the app's sandboxed storage on your iPhone. Specifically:

  • Your holdings, transactions, receipts, and goals never leave your device via network.
  • The app does not transmit, sync, or upload this data to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • If you enable iCloud Backup for your device, Apple's encrypted iCloud backup may include the app's data — this is governed by Apple's settings, not by XM Vista, and the data remains encrypted at rest.
  • If you delete XM Vista, all local data is permanently deleted along with the app.
  • You may export your data to CSV at any time from inside the app.

5. Permissions We Request

XM Vista requests only the iOS permissions needed to deliver its documented features:

  • Camera — used to attach photos to trades and snap receipts. Photos are stored locally and are never uploaded.
  • Photo Library — used to attach existing photos to trades and receipts. The app uses the minimal photo picker and does not request full library access unless you select "All Photos."

We do not request microphone, contacts, location, motion, health, Bluetooth, or background-tracking permissions.

6. Third-Party Services

XM Vista does not embed any third-party SDKs, frameworks, or remote services that process your data. In particular, the app does not use:

  • Firebase / Crashlytics / Google Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or Meta Pixel
  • Mixpanel, Amplitude, Segment, Heap, or PostHog
  • Sentry, Bugsnag, App Center, or any crash reporter
  • AdMob, AdSense, Unity Ads, or any ad network
  • Any brokerage, market-data, or quote provider (XM Vista does not connect to markets)

All chart rendering, calculations, and storage are performed on-device using first-party code.

7. Children's Privacy

XM Vista is intended for adults managing their own investments. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because we collect no data at all, this is a structural guarantee rather than a policy choice.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document and surface a notice inside the app on next launch. Continued use of XM Vista after such notice constitutes acceptance.

Because XM Vista does not collect contact information, we cannot email you about updates. You may re-read this page at any time.
