01.Frequently Asked Questions

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?
No. XM Vista stores every entry locally on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, and no sync. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it as a CSV file from inside the app. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Does XM Vista connect to my brokerage or place trades?
No. XM Vista is a journal, analyzer, simulator, and calculator. It does not connect to brokerages, does not execute trades, and does not custody funds. All entries are typed in by you. We do not provide investment advice — please consult a licensed advisor for personalized guidance.
How do I export my data?
Open Settings → Data inside the app. From there you can export Holdings, Transactions, or Receipts to CSV. The file is generated on-device and shared through the iOS share sheet — you choose where it goes (Files, Mail, AirDrop, etc.).
What happens to my data if I delete the app?
All data lives inside the app's sandboxed storage. Deleting the app permanently deletes the data along with it. If you think you might delete the app, export a CSV first.
The app is slow when I have many holdings — what can I do?
XM Vista is built to handle thousands of holdings, but very large journals can take a moment to render Canvas charts. Try the following: (1) close and reopen the app, (2) restart your iPhone, (3) make sure you are on the latest version. If the issue persists, contact support with your iOS version.
My camera permission was denied — how do I re-enable it?
Open the iOS Settings app → scroll to XM Vista → toggle Camera on. Then return to XM Vista and try attaching a photo again.
Will my data sync to a new iPhone?
Not automatically. XM Vista is intentionally offline-first. If you restore a new iPhone from an iCloud or iTunes backup, the app's data may be restored by iOS as part of that process. If not, contact support with your previous install date and we will do our best to help you migrate.
Does XM Vista show live market prices?
XM Vista is a journaling tool, not a market-data terminal. Prices shown are the ones you enter. This is deliberate — it keeps the app private, fast, and free of third-party data feeds.
How do I reset all my data?
Go to Settings → Data → Reset Everything. You will be asked to confirm. We recommend exporting a CSV backup first. The reset is irreversible.
Is there a Mac or iPad version?
XM Vista 1.0 is iPhone-only and requires iOS 16 or later. There are no current plans for iPad or macOS versions, but we are listening to user feedback.

02.Getting Started

1. Add your first holding

Open the Portfolio tab, tap the + button, and enter asset name, asset class, quantity, and cost basis.

2. Log a transaction

Open Journal → tap + → choose buy, sell, dividend, or split. Add tags and notes for richer analysis.

3. Try a calculator

Open Tools and explore Compound Interest, Loan, Currency, Retirement, DCA Simulator, or Risk Assessment.

4. Set a goal

Open Goals → + → pick savings, investment, or retirement. Add a deadline and XM Vista will suggest a pace.

03.Before You Contact Us

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  2. The XM Vista version number (shown on the Settings screen)
  3. A clear description of the issue and the screen on which it occurs
  4. Steps you have already tried (e.g., restart, reinstall)
